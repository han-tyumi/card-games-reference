# 0003. CC BY-SA 4.0 for the text, MIT for the code

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-01

## Context

The rules themselves are facts and belong to nobody. The write-ups are original
work with real effort in them. The project wants the text reused freely, wants
credit, wants improvements to come back, and wants to keep the option of
donations or advertising later.

## Decision

Text under CC BY-SA 4.0. Code under MIT.

## Consequences

Anyone may reuse the rules commercially, provided they credit Naibi and share
derivatives on the same terms — which is what makes a fork that improves an
entry come back rather than disappear. A NonCommercial clause was considered and
rejected: it would block ordinary uses like a games café printing a page, it is
notoriously vague about what counts, and it is not what "open" is generally
taken to mean. The share-alike condition does mean the text cannot be folded
into a proprietary product, which is deliberate.
